# NEST — Research Notes
Date: 2026-08-21

## Observations
- Urban Company currently markets home services with broad categories and an emphasis on verified professionals, hassle-free booking and transparent pricing.
- Urban Company's InstaHelp has expanded into quick-service housekeeping and reported 50,000+ daily bookings on a peak day in February 2026 and more than 1 million monthly delivered bookings by late March 2026.
- Belagavi already has local service businesses/platforms. UrbanCare lists electricians, plumbers, carpentry, painting, drivers, fabrication, bathroom cleaning and other services.
- Fixidu publicly announced a Belagavi launch and professional onboarding for electricians, plumbers, AC technicians, CCTV installers, carpenters and other home-service experts.

## Implication
The product cannot be differentiated merely by being local or by having an app. NEST needs a product-level wedge: problem-first discovery, transparent professional choice, persistent maintenance memory and repeat relationships.
