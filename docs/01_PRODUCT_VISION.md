# NEST — Product Vision
## Working name
NEST

## Working tagline
Your city's trusted service network.

## One-line description
NEST is a hyperlocal marketplace and personal maintenance assistant that helps people describe a problem, find and choose a trusted local professional, complete the job safely, and remember the home/vehicle/service history for next time.

## Product thesis
Do not build a smaller Urban Company. Build a different product:
- Urban Company is a broad, managed service marketplace.
- NEST is a local-first relationship marketplace plus a persistent maintenance layer.
- The core loop is: Describe → Understand → Compare → Choose → Book → Track → Approve → Review → Remember.

## Why now
Urban Company already has broad services, transparent pricing, verified professionals and fast-service offerings. Belagavi also has local service businesses and newer local apps. NEST therefore needs a distinct wedge rather than simply "Urban Company for Belagavi."

## Differentiation
1. Problem-first discovery: type, speak, photo/video instead of navigating categories.
2. Customer choice: compare professionals by trust, price, speed and experience.
3. Trust Score: transparent multi-factor trust profile, not just stars.
4. Your People: easily rebook professionals you trust.
5. Home Passport: persistent records for appliances, repairs, warranties and maintenance.
6. My Garage: extend the same maintenance model to vehicles.
7. Available Now: real local availability rather than only scheduled slots.
8. Professional storefronts: help independent local businesses build a digital presence and repeat customers.
9. Local language support: English first, then Kannada/Hindi/Hinglish.
10. Approval-based extras: no surprise additional charges.

## Long-term vision
NEST becomes the operating layer for maintaining a household and its vehicles:
- Who can help?
- Who do I trust?
- What was repaired?
- What is due next?
- What does it normally cost?
- Which professional did I use?
- Can they come now?

## Initial launch market
Belagavi, Karnataka.
Launch should be constrained to a small number of dense service zones before city-wide expansion.

## Initial customer segments
1. Apartment residents and families.
2. Young professionals living away from family.
3. Busy homeowners.
4. Students/young renters with urgent small jobs.
5. Small offices and shops as a later B2B segment.

## Initial professional segments
Independent electricians, plumbers, AC technicians, appliance technicians, CCTV/networking technicians, laptop technicians, car/bike detailers and selected cleaning providers.

## Product principles
- Simple before clever.
- Trust before growth.
- Local density before city breadth.
- Explicit prices/estimates.
- Customer approval for extras.
- Human support for disputes.
- AI assists; it does not make unsafe or irreversible decisions.
- Every screen should answer: "What can I do next?"

## Non-goals for V1
- Nationwide marketplace.
- Dozens of categories.
- Full social network.
- Lending/credit.
- Insurance marketplace.
- Autonomous AI diagnosis.
- Owning/operating a fleet of service professionals.
- Complex subscription products before repeat demand is proven.
