# NEST — Competitive Positioning
## Current competitive reality
Urban Company is a large national platform with broad service categories, transparent pricing, verified professionals and fast-service offerings. Its InstaHelp business has scaled to high booking volumes in selected metro markets.

Belagavi also has local service businesses/platforms such as UrbanCare and newer local marketplace efforts such as Fixidu.

## Therefore, NEST should NOT claim:
- "We are the first local service app."
- "We are the only app for home services."
- "We are faster than everyone."
- "Our professionals are verified" unless actually verified.
- "We are cheaper" unless measured.

## NEST positioning
For people who need trusted local help, NEST is the local service network that lets them describe the problem, choose the right professional, and keep a living record of their home and vehicle.

## Competitive wedge
1. Problem-first UX.
2. Professional choice.
3. Relationship/rebooking layer.
4. Home Passport.
5. Local professional storefront.
6. Trust Score transparency.
7. Available Now density.
8. Approval-based additional charges.

## Competitive strategy
Do not attack national platforms directly.
Win a dense local micro-market.
Build supply density.
Build repeat customer-professional relationships.
Use maintenance history to create retention.
Expand categories only after proving demand.

## Messaging
Primary:
"Tell us what you need. We'll find the right local professional."

Secondary:
"Your home. Your vehicle. Your trusted people."

## Proof points to build
Once live, measure and publish real metrics:
- average acceptance time
- average arrival time
- repeat booking rate
- on-time percentage
- dispute rate
- customer recommendation rate
- number of verified professionals
Never fabricate these metrics.
